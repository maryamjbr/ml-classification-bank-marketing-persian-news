## Bank Marketing dataset

`data/bank_marketing.csv` is the `bank-additional-full.csv` variant of the UCI
Bank Marketing dataset:

Moro, S., Rita, P., & Cortez, P. (2014). *Bank Marketing* [Dataset]. UCI Machine
Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5K306

UCI distributes this dataset under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Persian News dataset

The Persian News dataset used in the coursework was supplied by the course
teaching assistant. Its original publication source and redistribution license
were not included with the supplied course materials.

Because redistribution rights could not be verified, the dataset is not included
in the public repository and is not covered by the repository's MIT License.
Users with an authorized copy may place it locally at
`data/persian_news.csv` to run the corresponding notebook.
